Socket bases

Prismatic jewels only boost a Hologram skill that is already 3/3 on gear, from Lv. 3 to Lv. 8. A build needs one Prismatic per maxed skill; every other socket should be a colored stat jewel for your class. Once your line fodder makes an 18/18, use Orbs to move each piece's 3 lines onto the socket base of the same slot.

Useful stat colors for your class
STR classes such as Shade use Red, Green and Gray. Off-color sockets count as wasted.
